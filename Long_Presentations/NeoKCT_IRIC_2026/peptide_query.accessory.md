# Figure: how a peptide is queried

Three panels matching peptide_counts in NeoKCT's PeptideQuery.jl. Shorter than K=10: the 10-mers starting with the peptide (one prefix range) and the 10-mers ending with it (20 exact lookups for a 9-mer, 400 for an 8-mer), each group summed per sample, then the per-sample max. The end-anchored group was added 2026-11-20 to fix the C-terminal blind spot (peptide_counts anchor=:both). Exactly 10: the k-mer's own row. Longer than 10: the overlapping 10-mer tiles, elementwise minimum per sample (upper bound on reads carrying the whole peptide).

The k-mer sequences are real translations of one 33-nt read used in kct_overview.svg (MAGLKVTSAP, AGLKVTSAPQ) plus two neighbors of AGLKVTSAP in sorted order, and one made-up end-anchored neighbor (KAGLKVTSAP). All counts are illustrative. Generated by make_concept_figs.py.
