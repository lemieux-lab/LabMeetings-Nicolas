# Figure: how a peptide is queried

Three panels matching peptide_counts in NeoKCT's PeptideQuery.jl. Shorter than K=10: the prefix range of every 10-mer starting with the peptide, summed per sample. Exactly 10: the k-mer's own row. Longer than 10: the overlapping 10-mer tiles, elementwise minimum per sample (upper bound on reads carrying the whole peptide).

The k-mer sequences are real translations of one 33-nt read used in kct_overview.svg (MAGLKVTSAP, AGLKVTSAPQ) plus two neighbors of AGLKVTSAP in sorted order. All counts are illustrative. Generated by make_concept_figs.py.
