# Figure: the mascot

A CD8 T cell drawn by mascot() in make_concept_figs.py: round body, a ring of Y-shaped TCRs (none on the bottom), a nucleus hint, a small CD8 label, and two small solid eyes that slide toward whatever it's looking at. `mood` changes only brows and eye shape (angry, worried, surprised, perplexed, satisfied, squint), `sweat` adds a drop.

Where it appears: hauling a wobbly pile of books up from the basement, worried with a sweat drop, under the Disk column (7, mascot_books.svg). Angry with the knife and neutral with the ok hand in tsa_therapy (slide 3). mascot_worried with a sweat drop under the 622 TB bar (6). mascot_surprised with a "!" bubble looking at the green NeoKCT bar (11). mascot_perplexed holding a ruler (no tape-measure emoji exists) under 70% smaller (18). mascot_satisfied, eyes closed, under the 100% (20). mascot_stopwatch looking at ~46 min (23). mascot_telescope, one eye shut at the eyepiece, under the long-term row (26). Off duty with a coffee on Questions (tcell_questions.svg).

All mascot_*.svg share a 240 × 220 canvas with the cell at (120,130), placed at width 250 with {.absolute} in slide pixels (1920 × 1080), so the cell is the same size everywhere.
