# Figure: two workflows, backward vs. forward translation

Top lane, BamQuery (Ruiz Cuevas et al. 2023): peptide, back-translation to every coding DNA sequence, filtering to those present in GRCh38, reading every GTEx BAM at those loci, counts. Below it, one strip gives the exact product of codon degeneracy for AGLKVTSAP (4 × 4 × 6 × 2 × 4 × 4 × 6 × 4 × 4 = 294,912) and the expected count for 9-mers (~100,000) and 11-mers (~1.3 million) at 3.6 codons per residue, the degeneracy weighted by approximate human proteome amino-acid frequencies (UniProt). A second strip explains the genome filter and its cost. The unweighted mean is 3.05 (61 sense codons / 20 amino acids) and the geometric weighted mean, closer to a typical peptide, is 3.2.

Bottom lane, NeoKCT: reads translated forward (one 10-mer per 30-nt window), table, lookup, counts.

Real or derived: 294,912, the per-length averages, 199/550 (C5), ~730 CPU-h (published), 0.5 s / ~46 min (B4), 2,486 and 62.6 B. "About 10 days" for the build is Nicolas's figure. Generated by make_concept_figs.py.
