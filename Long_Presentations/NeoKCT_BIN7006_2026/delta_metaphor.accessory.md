# Figure: delta encoding as finish-line times

Left: four runners' clock times at a finish line, the first written in full and the rest as gaps (+5 s, +10 s, +27 s). The clock never runs backward, so gaps are always positive. Right: four sorted AA 10-mers, their real 5-bit AAAlphabet codes as decimal numbers, and the stored value (full number, then +5, +10, +27). The runner times are made up so their gaps match the real fragment gaps. Callout: every 256th entry is stored in full (DEFAULT_CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL), like a clock posted at each checkpoint, so any position is at most 255 additions away, about half the storage of plain 8-byte numbers.

Real: codes and differences. Simplified: the figure omits the overflow-to-checkpoint case (shown in NeoKCT/Figures/delta_array.svg). Generated by make_concept_figs.py.
